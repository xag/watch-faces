# Watch faces

Watch faces ready to install, sorted by the watch they are made for. Each watch's folder says how to install a face on it, and each face's folder has a picture of it and its files.

## Redmi Watch 5 Active

How to install: [redmi-watch-5-active](redmi-watch-5-active/README.md)

- [Two Skies](redmi-watch-5-active/two-skies/README.md): Paris above and Seoul below, each with its time and its sun or moon crossing its sky through the day; Paris also has its seconds, date, temperature and weather. In three looks: Arcade, Pop and Neon.
