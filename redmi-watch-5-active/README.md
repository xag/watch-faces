# Installing a face on the Redmi Watch 5 Active

Mi Fitness only installs faces from Xiaomi's own catalogue. A face from here goes on through **Notify for Xiaomi**, an Android app from the Play Store, free with ads, that talks to the watch in Mi Fitness's place. There is no way to do it from an iPhone.

The first time takes about ten minutes; after that, installing a face is steps 6 to 8 only.

## Before you start

- The watch must already be paired with Mi Fitness on the phone. **Never unpair it or reset it** afterwards: that changes the key Notify reads in step 1.
- **Make room:** the watch has room for little more than one face from here beside its own. Delete the downloaded faces you no longer want: press and hold the face on the watch, then delete it. The faces that came with the watch cannot be deleted.
- **Menu names** may appear in your phone's language and differ slightly from what is written here.

## Steps

1. **Save Mi Fitness's log.** In Mi Fitness, go to Profile, then About this app, tap the app's logo ten times and confirm. A zip file is saved in the Download/wearablelog folder of your phone. You do not need to open it.
2. **Install Notify for Xiaomi** from the Play Store.
3. **Connect Notify to the watch.** Open Notify, choose to import from the official app, then Import file, pick the Download/wearablelog folder, tap Use this folder and allow it. Notify finds the watch: tap its model.
4. **Let Notify hold the watch alone.** Only one app can be connected to the watch. When Notify asks, open Mi Fitness's settings from its button and turn off all of Mi Fitness's permissions. Do not uninstall Mi Fitness.
5. **Finish Notify's setup.** Allow what it asks; a test notification appears on the watch.
6. **Download the face's .bin file** to the phone.
7. **Install it.** In Notify, open the Device tab, then Watch faces, then the side menu, then Custom watch face (.bin); in French these are Appareil, Cadrans and Cadran personnalisé (.bin). Pick the file, tap Install, accept the compatibility warning, and tap Install again. Notify says when the transfer is finished.
8. **Choose it on the watch.** Press and hold the current face and swipe to the new one.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Not enough space":** delete another downloaded face on the watch and install again.
- **The watch reboots** when a face is chosen: it comes back with its usual face and nothing is lost. Delete that face and say which one it was.
- **Weather and temperature** come from the app connected to the watch, Notify, so keep it installed.
- **Gadgetbridge** does not work for this: its current version's install button never appears for a face, and the watch cuts the connection to the earlier one.
